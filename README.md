# Δv Sensitivity of Plane-Change Timing in Hohmann Transfers

This project examines how the timing of a plane change affects the total Δv required during a simplified orbital altitude transfer. I compared performing the plane change at transfer periapsis and transfer apoapsis.

I used circular-orbit velocity, the vis-viva equation, Hohmann-transfer burns, and the plane-change Δv equation. I completed the calculations and created the graphs in Excel.

For a transfer from 400 km to 800 km with a 47° plane change, performing the plane change at apoapsis required approximately 346 m/s less total Δv. For all outward-transfer cases tested under the simplified separate-burn model, the apoapsis case required less total Δv than the periapsis case.

## Scope and Limitations

This is an idealized two-body analysis with circular initial and final orbits and impulsive, separate burns. It does not model combined burns, atmospheric drag, orbital perturbations, or rendezvous phasing.
